# Privacy Policy for GymTracker

**Effective Date: August 10, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to GymTracker. This Privacy Policy explains how Donturex Development ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the GymTracker mobile application, including its optional Wear OS companion (together, the "App").

Donturex Development is responsible for the data-processing choices described in this Privacy Policy. GymTracker is designed to collect as little information as reasonably possible and to give you control over your workout data. Its core workout-tracking features are designed to work offline.

## Offline-first design

GymTracker is designed as an offline-first application. Your workout plans, detailed workout history, exercise logs, weights, repetitions, notes, Coach-related workout analysis, heart rate samples, and workout health summaries are primarily stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores these detailed workout records, and we do not have routine access to your personal workout logs or training history.

Some optional or supporting services may transmit limited information off your device. These include Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Analytics for Firebase, Meta App Events, Wear OS platform services, Android Health Services, Health Connect, Android system backup, and any storage provider you choose for an exported backup. Their processing is described below.

Enabling a platform feature does not mean that your complete GymTracker database is sent to us. Detailed workout data is transferred to another service only where the feature requires it and you choose to use that feature, such as exporting a completed workout to Health Connect, saving a backup to cloud storage, or allowing Android system backup.

## Information stored locally on your device

The App stores information that you create, enter, or record in order to provide its core functionality. This data is primarily stored locally on your phone and, while an active workout is in progress, may also be temporarily processed on a connected Wear OS watch.

This information may include:

- Workout plans, including training days, exercise selections, sets, repetitions, target durations, and weights
- Custom exercises, including names, muscle groups, exercise types, and instructions
- Workout history, including completed sessions, dates, times, exercises performed, sets, repetitions, weights, completion status, and time-based set durations
- Workout day name snapshots, so that history can keep the original workout day name even if the program day is later deleted or replaced
- Coach actions, such as progression suggestions, deloads, exercise variation suggestions, and automatically applied Coach changes
- App settings, such as language, theme, appearance mode, timers, warm-up settings, Coach settings, equipment preferences, analytics choice, Health Connect export settings, and onboarding or dialog preferences
- Wear OS workout-control data while an active workout is in progress
- Workout health data recorded with a compatible Wear OS watch, such as heart rate samples, average heart rate, maximum heart rate, estimated active calories, tracking duration, and workout health summaries
- Backup data created manually or automatically through a backup feature or platform backup service

The App uses this information to show your workout plan, record completed workouts, display history and statistics, provide locally generated Coach-like suggestions, show workout summaries, operate the Wear OS companion, and support optional Health Connect and backup features.

## Optional usage analytics with Google Analytics for Firebase

GymTracker uses Google Analytics for Firebase ("Firebase Analytics") to understand, at a general level, how the App and its features are used and where users may encounter problems.

Firebase Analytics collection is disabled by default in GymTracker. We ask you whether you want to allow usage analytics. If you decline or have not yet made a choice, GymTracker keeps Firebase Analytics collection disabled. The core App remains available without allowing Firebase Analytics.

If you allow usage analytics, GymTracker may send the following App events to Firebase Analytics:

- Visits to App screens, identified by a technical screen name such as Today, History, Settings, or Workout Session
- A workout being started
- A workout being completed
- A Coach suggestion being shown, including whether it concerns progression, exercise variation, or deloading
- A Coach suggestion being applied, including whether it was applied manually or automatically
- A workout being started through the Wear OS companion
- App lifecycle and engagement events, such as first open, app update, session start, and engagement time

These events do not contain the exercise name, workout-day name, detailed workout plan, sets, repetitions, weights, workout duration, workload, heart rate, calories, Health Connect records, notes, or the local workout-session identifier.

When Firebase Analytics is enabled, the Firebase Analytics SDK may also automatically process:

- A pseudonymous App-instance ID assigned to that installation of GymTracker
- App and device information, such as the App version, device model, operating-system version, and language
- Screen views, sessions, engagement, and other App lifecycle information
- Coarse location derived by Google from a masked IP address
- In-app purchase or subscription events, which may include a product ID, product name, price, and currency, when applicable

GymTracker does not set a Firebase user ID and does not send your name, email address, phone number, or GymTracker account identifier to Firebase Analytics. GymTracker does not provide a user-account system.

For Firebase Analytics, GymTracker disables Advertising ID collection and denies ad storage, ad user data, and ad-personalization consent. Firebase Analytics is not used by GymTracker to personalize advertisements.

The Firebase Analytics choice is separate from advertising-consent controls and from data processed by advertising or attribution SDKs, as described in the advertising and Meta App Events sections below.

You can change your Firebase Analytics choice later in the App's privacy settings. Turning it off stops future Firebase Analytics collection from GymTracker. It does not necessarily delete events that were collected before you turned it off. Previously collected data is retained according to the applicable Google Analytics settings and Google's retention rules.

Analytics information is pseudonymous rather than directly identifying: it is associated with an App installation identifier, not with your name or a GymTracker account. We use this information to measure feature usage, improve user flows, diagnose general usability issues, and guide App development. We do not use Firebase Analytics to inspect an individual user's detailed training history.

Google processes Firebase Analytics data on our behalf or in connection with its analytics services according to the applicable Google terms. For more information, see [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Wear OS companion and health tracking

GymTracker may include a Wear OS companion app. The phone app remains the source of truth for an active workout, while the Wear OS app works as a workout companion.

During an active workout, the Wear OS app may allow you to:

- View the active workout
- Mark sets as completed
- Edit weights
- Use rest timers
- Control warm-up tracking
- View workout health information

With a compatible Wear OS watch and the required permissions, the Wear OS app may use Android Health Services to record workout-related health data during an active workout. This may include:

- Heart rate
- Average heart rate
- Maximum heart rate
- Heart rate samples
- Estimated active calories
- Workout-tracking duration

This data is used to show live workout information on the watch, save workout health summaries in the phone app, show heart rate graphs, and optionally export compatible data to Health Connect if you enable that feature.

Workout-control messages and health summaries may be transferred directly between your watch and phone through Wear OS and Google Play services. We do not receive this data on a server operated by us.

Heart rate and calorie data may be incomplete or unavailable if the watch is not worn correctly, a sensor cannot read reliably, permissions are missing, or the device does not support the required data. Active-calorie values are estimates, and energy-expenditure estimates for strength training may be inaccurate.

## Health Connect export

GymTracker may offer optional Health Connect export as part of GymTracker Premium.

If you enable Health Connect export and grant the required permissions, GymTracker may write compatible completed workout data to Health Connect. This may include:

- Completed workout sessions
- Heart rate samples
- Estimated active calories

GymTracker currently uses Health Connect as an export destination. The current version does not read your Health Connect data back into GymTracker.

Other apps connected to Health Connect may use or display data exported by GymTracker depending on their own Health Connect support, permissions, privacy policies, and user interfaces. We cannot control how another app displays or interprets Health Connect data.

You can manage GymTracker's Health Connect permissions in your device's Health Connect settings. If you revoke permissions or disable Health Connect export in GymTracker, GymTracker will stop exporting new workout data to Health Connect.

Health Connect data is handled under applicable Android and Google platform rules. GymTracker does not use Health Connect data for advertising or send it to Firebase Analytics or Meta App Events.

## Optional backups, Android system backup, exports, and imports

The App includes optional backup, export, and import features.

When you choose to export or back up your data, the App creates a backup file, such as a JSON file, that may contain your workout plans, exercises, workout history, supported settings, session data, Coach action logs, workout health summaries, and other data supported by the backup format.

Backups may be created manually or automatically if you enable GymTracker's automatic-backup feature. An automatic backup keeps a backup file selected by you up to date according to the selected backup settings.

The backup file is saved to a location you choose. This may be local storage on your device or a cloud-storage location, such as Google Drive, if you select it through the Android file picker. GymTracker does not automatically send this file to us or to a server operated by us.

Android may also back up or transfer some locally stored App data through its system backup or device-transfer features, depending on your device, Google account, Android version, and backup settings. Such system backup is controlled by Android and the platform provider, not by Donturex Development. You can manage system backup through your device settings.

You are responsible for the security and privacy of exported backup files. If you store a file in cloud storage, send it to another person, or move it to another device, the storage provider or recipient may be able to access it depending on your choices and the service used.

Importing a backup is initiated by you. Depending on the App's current import behavior and the contents of the backup, importing may replace existing local App data.

## Data storage and security

Your detailed workout data is primarily stored locally on your device. We take reasonable measures in the App's implementation to protect the data used by the App, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information sent to Firebase Analytics is encrypted in transit using transport-layer security according to Google's documentation. Other platform providers use their own security measures.

The overall security of your data also depends on your device, account, backup location, and choices. You are responsible for:

- Keeping your device secure
- Using a screen lock or other device-security features
- Keeping the operating system and App updated
- Protecting exported backup files
- Choosing secure storage locations for backups
- Managing Android system-backup settings
- Managing Health Connect permissions
- Managing permissions granted to other apps connected to Health Connect

If you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device settings, locally stored App data may be deleted. Cloud backups, exported files, platform analytics, advertising data, purchase records, and Health Connect records are controlled separately and may not be deleted at the same time.

## Data retention

- **Local App data:** Remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, import a backup that replaces it, or uninstall the App.
- **Exported backup files:** Remain in the location you selected until you or the relevant storage provider delete them.
- **Android system backups:** Are retained and deleted according to your Android, Google-account, device-transfer, and platform-backup settings.
- **Health Connect data:** Remains in Health Connect according to Health Connect behavior and your settings. You can manage or delete it through Health Connect or supported system settings.
- **Firebase Analytics data:** User-level event data is retained according to the configured Google Analytics retention settings and applicable Google retention rules. Turning off analytics stops future collection but does not automatically remove previously collected events. Aggregated reporting may be retained for longer periods.
- **Diagnostics:** Crash, performance, and technical information is retained by platform providers according to their settings and retention policies.
- **Advertising and attribution data:** Is retained by Google, Meta, or another applicable provider according to its privacy policy, settings, and legal obligations.
- **Billing and purchase information:** Is retained by Google and, where applicable, by us to the extent necessary to provide entitlement, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud.
