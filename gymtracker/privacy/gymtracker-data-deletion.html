GymTracker – Data Deletion Request

Information about deleting your GymTracker data and submitting a privacy or data deletion request.

GymTracker does not use user accounts

GymTracker does not provide user accounts and does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Data stored on your device

Most GymTracker workout data is stored locally on your device. This may include workout plans, workout history, exercises, weights, repetitions, notes, settings and workout health information recorded through supported features.

You can remove locally stored GymTracker data by:

  • Deleting supported workouts, exercises or other data inside the app
  • Clearing GymTracker's app data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling GymTracker from your device

Request deletion of data

You may submit a privacy or data deletion request to Donturex Development by email.

Email: gymtracker@donturexdev.fi

Please state that your request concerns GymTracker and briefly describe what data you would like deleted.

Send data deletion request

Third-party services

GymTracker uses or may interact with third-party services such as Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Meta App Events, Health Connect and Android platform services.

Some information processed by these services may be retained according to the service provider's own privacy policy, retention settings and legal obligations.

A deletion request sent to Donturex Development does not necessarily delete information independently controlled by Google, Meta or another third-party service.

Backups and Health Connect

Files that you export or save as backups remain in the storage location selected by you until you delete them.

Data exported to Health Connect can be managed and deleted through Health Connect or the supported Android system settings.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how GymTracker handles data, please see the GymTracker Privacy Policy .
