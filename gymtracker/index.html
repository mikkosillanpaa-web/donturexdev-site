About GymTracker

GymTracker helps you build a gym program, track workouts, follow progress and stay consistent. The app combines workout logging, equipment-aware program creation, exercise instructions, rest timers, timed sets, workout summaries, visual history, Wear OS workout control, optional heart rate tracking and local Coach logic for progression, deloads and exercise variation.

Program

Program creation & equipment preferences

  • Create workout programs based on goal, level, schedule and training split.
  • Use equipment preferences: Prefer, OK, Avoid and Off.
  • Built-in exercise library with machines, cables, dumbbells, barbells, Smith machine, kettlebells, resistance bands and bodyweight movements.
  • Smarter program generation helps create more practical training plans for your gym setup.
During workout

Track sets, timers and workout changes

  • Log sets, reps, weights and timed sets directly during the workout.
  • Built-in rest timer with notification and vibration support.
  • Add, remove or replace exercises during a workout — only for this session or update the program.
  • Adapt the current workout for a temporary home or outdoor session without changing your program.
Wear OS

Workout companion on your watch

  • View the active workout and control sets from a Wear OS watch.
  • Edit weights, run rest timers and control warm-ups during the workout.
  • Track heart rate and estimated active calories with a compatible Wear OS watch.
  • The phone remains the source of truth while the watch works as a convenient workout companion.
Health

Heart rate, summaries & Health Connect

  • Workout summaries can show average heart rate, max heart rate and estimated active calories.
  • Workout details can show a heart rate graph when data is recorded.
  • Export compatible completed workouts, heart rate samples and active calories to Health Connect when enabled.
  • Other apps may show Health Connect data depending on their own support.
Coach

Progression, deloads and exercise guidance

  • Coach logic suggests progression, load changes, deloads and exercise variations.
  • History-based suggestions use the exercises you actually performed.
  • Return-to-training support can lighten one workout after a longer break.
  • Localized exercise instructions explain purpose, setup, execution and safety.
History

Clear workout history

  • Weekly overview with completed workouts.
  • Monthly calendar with visual workout markers.
  • Workout summaries show sets, target completion, duration, volume, muscle group load and available health data.
  • Tap a calendar day to open workout details.

How GymTracker works

GymTracker is built around clear training flows: creating your program, logging workouts, optionally using your watch during training, and reviewing progress through summaries, history and Coach feedback.

1. Create your program

  • Choose your goal, training level, weekly schedule and training split.
  • Set equipment preferences so the program matches your gym setup.
  • Preview the generated program before saving it.
  • You can still edit training days and exercises manually.

2. Log your workouts

  • Start a workout from the Workout tab or a training day detail screen.
  • Tap set chips to mark sets completed; timed exercises can use a built-in timer.
  • Use the rest timer between working sets, or turn it off from settings.
  • Add, remove or replace exercises during the workout when plans change.

3. Use Wear OS during training

  • Start or continue the workout on your phone, then open GymTracker on your watch.
  • Mark sets completed, edit weights, control warm-ups and use rest timers from the watch.
  • With a compatible watch, GymTracker can record heart rate and estimated active calories during the workout.

4. Review history & Coach feedback

  • The History dashboard shows your training week, monthly calendar, summaries and recent workouts.
  • Workout summaries and details can include health data and a heart rate graph when available.
  • Coach logic uses recent performance to suggest progression, deloads or exercise variations.

Designed for gym training

GymTracker is primarily a gym training app, but it also supports temporary home or outdoor workout adaptations when you cannot get to the gym.

  • Track regular gym sessions.
  • Use equipment-aware program generation.
  • Adapt a single workout for bodyweight, bands or dumbbells.
  • Keep your long-term program intact.

Privacy

GymTracker is designed to respect your privacy. You can log and review your training without creating an account.

GymTracker does not require an account to log workouts. Workout data is stored locally on your device. GymTracker does not upload your personal workout logs to its own servers.

Health Connect export is optional. If enabled, GymTracker can write compatible completed workout sessions, heart rate samples and estimated active calories to Health Connect. Other apps may use or display that data depending on their own Health Connect support.

Ads and purchases are handled through Google services. GymTracker does not share your personal workout logs with advertising partners.

Support & contact

If you encounter issues, have questions about how the app works or want to suggest features, you can contact the developer directly.

Is GymTracker free?

GymTracker can be downloaded for free. The app includes a 30-day Premium trial. After the trial, you can continue using the free ad-supported version or choose optional GymTracker Premium. Core workout tracking remains available in the free version.

What does Premium include?

Premium includes no ads, Wear OS workout support, heart rate and estimated active calorie tracking with a compatible watch, Health Connect export, automatic backup and personalized cooldown guidance.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required to use GymTracker. Workout logs and settings are stored locally on your device. Internet is mainly needed for updates, ads, Google Play Billing, Health Connect setup, backup locations such as cloud storage and related Google Play services.

Which devices are supported?

GymTracker is designed for Android phones running Android 8.0 or newer. The Wear OS companion is available for compatible Wear OS watches.
