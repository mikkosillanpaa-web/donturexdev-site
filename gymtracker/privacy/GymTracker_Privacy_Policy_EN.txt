Privacy Policy for GymTracker
Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Introduction
Welcome to GymTracker. This Privacy Policy explains how Donturex Development (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our mobile application, GymTracker (the “App”).

Your privacy is important to us. GymTracker is designed to collect as little information as possible and to give you control over your workout data. The App is built primarily as an offline-first workout tracking application.

Offline-first design
GymTracker is designed as an offline-first application. Your personal workout data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your workout plans, workout history, exercise logs, weights, repetitions, notes, Coach-related workout analysis, heart rate samples, or workout health summaries.

This means that we do not have access to your personal workout logs or training history.

Some optional platform services, such as Google Play services, advertising, billing, consent management, Wear OS platform services, or Health Connect, may process certain technical, advertising, purchase, permission, diagnostic, or health-related data as described in this Privacy Policy. These services do not receive your personal workout logs from us unless you explicitly enable or use a feature that transfers data to that platform, such as Health Connect export.

Information stored locally on your device
The App stores information that you create, input, or record in order to provide its core functionality. This data is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

Workout plans, including training days, exercise selections, sets, repetitions, target durations, and weights
Custom exercises, including names, muscle groups, exercise types, and instructions
Workout history, including completed sessions, dates, times, exercises performed, sets, repetitions, weights, completed status, and time-based set durations
Workout day name snapshots, so that history can keep the original workout day name even if the program day is later deleted or replaced
Coach actions, such as progression suggestions, deloads, exercise variation suggestions, or automatically applied Coach changes
App settings, such as language, theme, appearance mode, rest timer settings, warm-up settings, Coach settings, equipment preferences, Health Connect export settings, and onboarding or intro dialog preferences
Wear OS workout control data while an active workout is in progress
Workout health data recorded with a compatible Wear OS watch, such as heart rate samples, average heart rate, maximum heart rate, estimated active calories, and workout health summaries
Optional backup data created manually or automatically to a file you choose
This information is used by the App to show your workout plan, track your progress, display history, calculate statistics, provide local Coach-like suggestions, show workout summaries, and support optional Wear OS and Health Connect features.

Information we do not directly collect
We do not directly collect personal identifiers such as:

Your name
Your email address
Your phone number
Your physical address
Your contacts
Precise GPS location data
Your personal workout logs on our servers
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal workout data.

However, if you use features that rely on third-party platform services, such as advertising, Google Play Billing, app distribution, diagnostics, consent management, Health Connect, or Wear OS services, those services may process certain technical, device, advertising, purchase, diagnostic, consent-related, permission-related, or health-related data according to their own privacy policies and applicable law. More information is provided below.

Wear OS companion and health tracking
GymTracker may include a Wear OS companion app. The phone app remains the source of truth for the active workout, while the Wear OS app works as a workout companion.

During an active workout, the Wear OS app may allow you to:

View the active workout
Mark sets as completed
Edit weights
Use rest timers
Control warm-up tracking
View workout health data
With a compatible Wear OS watch and required permissions, the Wear OS app may use Android Health Services to record workout-related health data during an active workout. This may include:

Heart rate
Average heart rate
Maximum heart rate
Heart rate samples
Estimated active calories
Workout tracking duration
This data is used to show live workout information on the watch, save workout health summaries in the phone app, show heart rate graphs, and optionally export compatible data to Health Connect if you enable that feature.

Heart rate and calorie data may be incomplete or unavailable if the watch is not worn correctly, the sensor cannot read reliably, permissions are missing, or the device does not support the required data. Active calorie values are estimates. In strength training, energy expenditure estimates may be inaccurate.

Health Connect export
GymTracker may offer an optional Health Connect export feature as part of GymTracker Premium.

If you enable Health Connect export and grant the required permissions, GymTracker may write compatible completed workout data to Health Connect. This may include:

Completed workout sessions
Heart rate samples
Estimated active calories
GymTracker currently uses Health Connect as an export destination. The current version does not read your Health Connect data back into GymTracker.

Other apps connected to Health Connect may use or display the data exported by GymTracker depending on their own Health Connect support, permissions, privacy policies, and user interface. We cannot control how other apps display or interpret Health Connect data.

You can manage Health Connect permissions in your device’s Health Connect settings. If you revoke permissions or disable Health Connect export in GymTracker, GymTracker will stop exporting new workout data to Health Connect.

Optional backups and exports
The App includes optional backup, export, and import features.

When you choose to export or back up your data, the App creates a backup file, such as a JSON file, that may contain your workout plans, exercises, workout history, settings supported by the backup format, session data, Coach action logs, workout health summaries, and other supported App data.

Backups may be created manually by you or automatically if you enable automatic backup. Automatic backup keeps one backup file selected by you up to date according to the selected backup settings.

The backup file is saved to a location that you choose. This location may be local storage on your device or your own cloud storage location, such as Google Drive, if available through the Android file picker. The App does not automatically send this file to us or to any server operated by us.

You are responsible for the security and privacy of exported backup files. If you store the file in cloud storage, send it to another person, or move it to another device, that storage provider or recipient may have access to the file depending on your actions and the service used.

When you import a backup, the import is initiated by you. Depending on the App’s current backup behavior, importing may replace the existing local data in the App.
